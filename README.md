# Vridhi 🌱

**Understand your money. Plan your future. Grow your wealth.**

Vridhi is a personal finance, budgeting, and portfolio management application designed to help users understand their financial position today and make better decisions about their financial future.

Instead of focusing only on expense tracking, Vridhi aims to provide a unified view of a user's **income, expenses, budgets, savings, investments, assets, liabilities, and financial goals**.

> **Where am I financially today, and where am I heading?**

Vridhi aims to help users answer that question.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Managing personal finances often involves multiple disconnected tools.

A user might use:

- a budgeting app for expenses
- spreadsheets for investments
- brokerage applications for stocks
- separate applications for mutual funds
- calculators for financial goals
- notes or spreadsheets for tracking net worth

While these tools provide individual pieces of information, they often fail to provide a complete picture of a person's financial life.

Knowing how much you spent last month is useful.

Understanding how your **spending, savings, investments, liabilities, and future decisions collectively affect your wealth** is far more valuable.

Vridhi aims to bridge that gap.

---

## 💡 The Vision

Vridhi is envisioned as a **Personal Wealth & Financial Planning Platform**.

The application aims to help users:

- Track income and expenses
- Create and manage budgets
- Understand spending patterns
- Track assets and liabilities
- Monitor net worth
- Manage investment portfolios
- Track financial goals
- Understand asset allocation
- Visualize wealth growth over time
- Model future financial scenarios
- Make more informed financial decisions

The long-term goal is to move beyond answering:

> "How much did I spend this month?"

toward answering questions such as:

> "When could I reach ₹1 crore in net worth?"

> "Can I afford to buy a car next year?"

> "What happens if I increase my investments by ₹20,000 per month?"

> "How would taking a six-month career break affect my financial goals?"

## ✨ Core Features

### 💰 Money Management

Manage everyday personal finances through:

- Income tracking
- Expense tracking
- Transaction categorization
- Accounts
- Monthly budgets
- Spending analysis

### 📊 Financial Dashboard

Get a consolidated overview of:

- Income
- Expenses
- Savings
- Savings rate
- Budget utilization
- Recent transactions
- Financial trends

### 🏦 Net Worth Tracking

Track assets and liabilities including:

- Bank accounts
- Cash
- Fixed deposits
- Stocks
- Mutual funds
- Retirement investments
- Gold
- Other assets
- Loans and liabilities

Vridhi can then provide a historical view of how the user's net worth changes over time.

### 📈 Portfolio Management

Bring investments together to understand:

- Current portfolio value
- Investment returns
- Asset allocation
- Portfolio diversification
- Historical performance
- Investment contributions

### 🎯 Financial Goals

Create and track goals such as:

- Emergency fund
- Home purchase
- Vehicle purchase
- Travel
- Education
- Retirement
- Custom financial goals

### 🔮 Financial Simulator

A future capability of Vridhi will allow users to model financial decisions before making them.

For example:

```text
Current monthly investment: ₹30,000
Projected ₹1 Cr milestone:   2034

Increase investment:        +₹20,000/month
New monthly investment:      ₹50,000
Projected ₹1 Cr milestone:   2031
```

This enables users to understand how today's decisions could affect their long-term financial position.

## 🧠 Financial Intelligence

Over time, Vridhi aims to introduce intelligent financial insights such as:

- Spending trend detection
- Recurring expense identification
- Unusual spending detection
- Savings-rate analysis
- Portfolio concentration insights
- Goal progress analysis

## 🌱 Why "Vridhi"?

**Vridhi** comes from the Sanskrit word **वृद्धि (Vṛddhi)**, meaning **growth, increase, progress, or prosperity**.

The name represents the core philosophy of the application:

> **Understand your money today so you can grow your wealth tomorrow.**
